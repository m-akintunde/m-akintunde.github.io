About.

I'm Michael Akintunde. I am a member of the AI Research Centre at City St George's, University of London, in the Department of Computer Science, where I work as a Lecturer.

Previously, I worked in the Department of Informatics at King's College London as a Postdoctoral Researcher as part of the UKRI Trustworthy Autonomous Systems (TAS) Hub, advised by Prof. Luc Moreau. Before that, I worked full-time in the financial services industry.

I obtained my PhD in Computing from Imperial College in the Safe Artificial Intelligence Lab (Formerly known as the Verification of Autonomous Systems Group), supervised by Prof. Alessio Lomuscio.

My research interests are primarily in the area of formal verification, particularly for multi-agent systems, and for establishing the correctness of software generated using machine learning.

Software.

NANESVerify (Neural Agent operating on a Non-deterministic Environment System Verify), a software package for performing MILP-based verification for agent-environment systems where the agent is controlled by ReLU-activated feed-forward neural networks, the environment is non-deterministic with branching temporal evolutions, and specifications are expressed in a bounded variant of CTL. (paper)

RNSVerify (Recurrent Neural System Verify), an extension of NSVerify to handle recurrent neural networks. (paper)

NSVerify (Neural System Verify), a software package for performing reachability analysis on neural agent-environment systems implemented by ReLU feed-forward neural networks. (paper)
